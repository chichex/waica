## Contexto

Playing `examples/isometric` (v0.8.0) surfaced four problems: the hero's left/right animations are inverted, touching the orc "does nothing", there is no way to attack, and the camera follow feels abrupt. The grill established the facts behind each one:

- **The inversion is art, not code.** `packages/archetype-isometric/assets/ATTRIBUTION.md` composed `ne/e/se` from Puny Characters source rows 5/6/7, which face **west**. Verified on the source sheets: the row order is `0 s · 1 se · 2 e · 3 ne · 4 n · 5 nw · 6 w · 7 sw`, with pixel-exact mirror pairs 1↔7, 2↔6, 3↔5 in all three character files. The directional contract (`packages/archetype-isometric/src/animation.ts`), `IsoMotor` (`packages/behaviors/src/iso-motor.ts`), the e2e leg and every test assert clip names and `flipX`, never absolute orientation — exactly the gap the previous spec (`archetype-isometric.md`, CA-10) declared not mechanically assertable, and the same bug topdown fixed in `a807897`.
- **Damage works but is invisible.** Probed with the shipped `ISOMETRIC_SCENE` in happy-dom: each orc contact calls `Health.damage(1)` (1 s i-frames), at 0 HP the player enters `dead` for 0.8 s and respawns at the origin with full health. Nothing shows it: `stats.json` only has `points`, nothing listens to the `damage` event, there is no blink, knockback or hurt animation.
- **No attack exists anywhere.** Melee was deferred by the topdown and isometric handoffs. The orc is `Hitbox + Hazard` without `Health` (`packages/archetype-isometric/src/prefabs.ts:107-130`); `Hazard.onCollide` only reacts to the player and only stomps with a `PlatformerMotor` (`packages/behaviors/src/hazard.ts:71-96`), so nothing lets the player damage an enemy. The Puny pack (CC0, originals in `~/Sync/workspace/waica-art-src/iso-kit-a/`) has sword, hurt and death columns in all eight rows.
- **The camera feel comes from `CAMERA_DEFAULTS`** (`packages/engine/src/camera.ts:45-55`): deadzone 2×2.5, `smoothing: 6`, `lookahead: 1.5` applied as a step once `|vx| > 1`, plus the iso scene's `lookaheadY: 1`. Target and velocity are projected correctly (`game.ts:374-400`). The scene block can override every one of those fields; the engine defaults and the other archetypes stay untouched.
- **`Patrol` moves along a logical axis and reports no facing** (`packages/behaviors/src/patrol.ts:41-52`), so the orc plays the plain south-facing `walk` clip mirrored with `scale.x` while walking a screen diagonal.

Grill decisions 1–10 are confirmed and fixed: orc facing is in scope; the attack is melee; all four kinds of damage feedback; camera tuning in the iso scene only; the attack ships in the isometric archetype; the orc gets `Health 2`, dies and disappears; the feedback is generic in behaviors/engine but wired only in the iso demo; a short stun on hit; `Patrol` facing wherever a directional contract is installed; one spec, one PR.

### Attack

- **CA-3** (ALTA): `ISOMETRIC_BINDINGS` gains `attack: ['KeyX', 'KeyJ']` and `ISOMETRIC_ACTION_LABELS` gains `attack: 'Attack'`; `examples/isometric/src/controls.json` carries the same binding (it is hand-maintained: `sync-scene` does not write it). `controls.test.ts` pins both objects; the conformance suite's bindings/labels parity rule passes; `projectFiles(…, 'isometric')` and MCP `createProject` emit the binding in `controls.json` (template snapshots regenerated, `create-project.test.ts` byte-parity stays green).
- **CA-4** (ALTA): New component `MeleeAttack` in `packages/behaviors/src/melee-attack.ts` (`componentName: 'MeleeAttack'`, params `damage` (default 1, min 0, max 20), `range` (default 1, world units in front of the attacker) and `width` (default 1)). It exposes `strike(facing: string): Entity[]`: it maps the screen facing's sign pair to a logical unit vector with `screenInputToLogical`, builds a logical-space rectangle of `range × width` centred `range/2` in front of `entity.position`, and for every other alive entity with `Hitbox` **and** `Health` whose hitbox overlaps that rectangle (`collisionOverlap`, hitbox offsets honoured) calls `health.damage(this.damage, this.entity)` exactly once per `strike` call; entities without `Health` (villager, crates) and the attacker itself are never touched. Unit harness in the style of `hazard-damage.test.ts`: hits an orc in front (facing `e` under the same logical mapping the motor uses), misses an orc behind, misses beyond `range`, ignores the attacker and Health-less entities, deals exactly one hit per call, and `strike` returns the entities it damaged. `MeleeAttack` is registered in `ISOMETRIC_REGISTRY_DATA.components`, appears in `registry-data.test.ts`'s `EXPECTED_DEFAULTS` with its exact defaults, and has a row in the editor's `script-sources.ts` (`script-sources.test.ts` updated).
- **CA-5** (ALTA): The shared grid player graph (`createGridPlayerRole`, hence `ISO_PLAYER_STATE_GRAPH` **and** `TOPDOWN_PLAYER_STATE_GRAPH`) gains an `attack` state entered by `input:attack` from `idle` and `walk`, leaving to `idle` on `timer:0.3`. The role's `attack` state code: `onEnter` halts the motor and, when the entity has `MeleeAttack`, calls `strike(motor.facing)` once; `onUpdate` is an explicit no-op body (no input, no movement — the documented `default` fallback trap in `grid-player-role.ts:84-86`), `interactUpdate` keeps running through `'*'`. Unit (`iso-player-states.test.ts` + a grid-role test): pressing `attack` while idle enters `attack`, the motor velocity is 0 for the whole state, `strike` is called exactly once with the current facing, the state returns to `idle` after 0.3 s, and pressing `attack` during `attack` does nothing. The iso player prefab (`prefabs.ts`, template, example) gains `{ type: 'MeleeAttack' }`; the topdown player prefab is **not** changed (no `MeleeAttack`, no `attack` binding: the state is present but unreachable there and its clip resolves to `idle` through the contract fallback — asserted by the conformance rule).

### Damage feedback (generic in behaviors, wired in the iso demo)

- **CA-6** (ALTA): `Health` gains: (a) param `stat` (`ref: 'stat'`, default `''`): when non-empty, `current` is published with `game.stats.set(stat, current)` on `onReady` and after every accepted `damage`/`heal`; (b) on every accepted non-lethal hit it emits `'damage'` as today **and** signals `'hurt'` to a sibling `StateMachine` (fire-and-forget, harmless when the graph has no `signal:hurt` edge) after recording the transient `lastDamageSource: Entity | undefined`; (c) while `invulnerable > 0` it toggles `entity.node.visible` every 0.1 s (blink) and restores `visible = true` the frame the window closes, exposing the transient `blinking: boolean` for snapshots. Unit (`health.test.ts`): stat published on ready and on each hit, no stat call when `stat` is `''`, `hurt` signalled on a non-lethal hit and **not** on the lethal one (death wins), `lastDamageSource` is the hit's source, `node.visible` alternates during the window and ends `true`, and an entity with `invulnerability: 0` never blinks.
- **CA-7** (ALTA): The shared grid player graph gains a `hurt` state: `'*'` transitions become `[ { on: 'signal:death', to: 'dead' }, { on: 'signal:hurt', to: 'hurt' } ]` (death listed first so it wins the race), and `hurt` leaves to `idle` on `timer:0.3`. Role code: `onEnter` reads `Health.lastDamageSource` and sets the motor's logical velocity to a unit vector pointing from the source's position to the player's (falls back to the reverse of the current facing when there is no source) times `knockbackSpeed` (a `GridMotor` param, default 8, min 0, max 30); `onUpdate` only calls `motor.step(dt)` (no input), so the push is resolved against Solids and the Tilemap; `onExit` halts the motor. Unit: a hit from an entity at logical `(+1, 0)` moves the player toward `−x` for 0.3 s and stops it, input is ignored while hurt, `hurt` is interrupted by death (`dead` is reached from `hurt` on a lethal hit), and a `hurt` entered with no source pushes backwards from the facing.
- **CA-8** (ALTA): `ISOMETRIC_UI` gains the piece `health` (`♥ {{health}}`, styled like `crate-counter`, top-right) and `ISOMETRIC_SCENE.ui` becomes `['crate-counter', 'health']`. The iso player prefab's `Health` sets `stat: 'health'`. `packages/editor/template/src/stats.json` and `examples/isometric/src/stats.json` declare `health: 3` (the template file is shared by the three archetypes — `template.ts:86`, `create-project.ts:106` — and already carries the unused `lives: 3`; adding `health` there avoids the MCP `undeclared-stat` warning on generated projects). `scene-default.test.ts` pins the `ui` list; the conformance rule "every scene ui piece is registered" passes; `template.test.ts` (the piece is written to `src/ui/health.html`, the `ui` list includes it) and its six snapshots are regenerated; `examples/{platformer,topdown}/src/stats.json` are **not** touched.
